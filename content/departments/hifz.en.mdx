title: "Hifz Department"
---

The Hifz Department guides students to the complete memorization of the Holy Qur'an — 6,236 verses across 114 surahs — with correct tajweed (rules of recitation) and retention.

## Our Approach

Each student is individually assessed and placed on a personalised memorization schedule. Daily sessions combine new memorization (*sabaq*), revision of recent passages (*sabaq para*), and long-term revision (*manzil*).

## Tajweed Certification

All students graduate with a verified tajweed certificate. Advanced students may pursue *ijazah* in the Hafs 'an 'Asim recitation — an authorised chain of transmission traced back to the Prophet ﷺ.

## Duration

Most students complete Hifz in **two to four years** depending on ability and dedication. Younger students who begin early often complete in two years.

## Support

Boarding accommodation is available for students who travel from outside Dhaka. A supportive, structured environment ensures students can focus entirely on their memorization goals.
