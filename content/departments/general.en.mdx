title: "General Classes"
---

The General Classes programme integrates the national Bangladeshi curriculum with a structured Islamic studies component, giving students a sound education in both religious and worldly knowledge.

## Subjects Covered

**National Curriculum:** Bangla, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, ICT

**Islamic Studies (integrated):** Qur'an reading and basic tajweed, Islamic studies, Arabic language foundations, Fiqh of worship (*ibadat*)

## Who It's For

General Classes are ideal for students who:
- Are not yet ready to commit to full Kitab or Hifz study
- Wish to pursue national board examinations (SSC/HSC pathway)
- Want a balanced grounding in deen and dunya before choosing a specialisation

## Outcome

Students complete JSC/SSC-equivalent board exams while simultaneously building a strong Islamic foundation. Many graduates continue into the Kitab or Hifz tracks, or into mainstream higher education with a distinct Islamic character.
