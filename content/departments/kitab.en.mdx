The Kitab Department is the academic heart of Jamiatun Noor Al Islamia. Students undertake a rigorous multi-year curriculum in the classical Islamic sciences, following the traditional *dars-e-nizami* framework adapted for contemporary learners.

## Curriculum Highlights

- **Fiqh** — Islamic jurisprudence from the Hanafi school, studied through primary texts
- **Hadith** — Study of the six major hadith collections with chains of narration (*isnad*)
- **Tafsir** — Qur'anic exegesis drawing on classical and contemporary commentaries
- **Arabic Language** — Grammar (*nahw*), morphology (*sarf*), rhetoric (*balagha*)
- **Usul al-Fiqh** — Principles of Islamic legal theory
- **Aqeedah** — Islamic theology and creed

## Duration

The complete Kitab track spans **eight years**. Students who complete the full programme receive the *Dawra al-Hadith* certificate — the highest credential in the traditional seminary system.

## Faculty

All Kitab courses are taught by credentialed scholars holding *ijazah* (authorised transmission) in their respective subjects, ensuring an unbroken chain of knowledge transmission.
