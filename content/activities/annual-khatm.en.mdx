The annual Khatm al-Bukhari is one of the most revered traditions at Jamiatun Noor Al Islamia. This year's gathering brought together senior scholars, faculty, graduating students, and community members to mark the completion of Sahih al-Bukhari — the most authentic collection of Prophetic narrations.

## The Ceremony

The day began with the recitation of Qur'anic verses followed by a scholarly address by Principal Mufti Abdullah Rahman on the importance of hadith sciences in contemporary Islamic practice.

Students who completed the study of Sahih al-Bukhari this academic year were honoured with certificates and du'a from senior scholars.

## Community Impact

Events like these reinforce the institution's commitment to preserving classical Islamic scholarship while making it accessible to the next generation. Over 200 community members attended the open gathering.
