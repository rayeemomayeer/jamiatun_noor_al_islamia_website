Six students from Jamiatun Noor Al Islamia competed in the Regional Inter-Madrasa Islamic Knowledge Competition, representing the institution against fifteen other institutions from across Dhaka Division.

## Competition Overview

Topics covered classical fiqh, Qur'anic tafsir, and contemporary Islamic ethics. Rounds included written exams, oral recitation, and moderated debate.

## Results

The institution placed second overall. Maulana Sayeed Ahmad commended all participants: *"The goal is not only to win but to demonstrate that our students engage with Islamic knowledge with both depth and confidence."*

Plans are underway to host next year's regional competition at the institution.
