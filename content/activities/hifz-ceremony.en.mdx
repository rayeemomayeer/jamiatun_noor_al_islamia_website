This year's Hifz Graduation Ceremony was a joyful milestone for the institution, as forty students — ranging in age from ten to eighteen — completed the memorization of the entire Holy Qur'an under the guidance of Hafiz Ibrahim Khan and the Hifz faculty.

## The Achievement

Memorizing the Qur'an is among the highest honours in Islamic tradition. Each student invested between two and four years of dedicated daily practice, guided by patient teachers who ensured precision in both memorization and tajweed.

## Recognition

Graduates received *ijazah* certificates and were honoured in the presence of their families. Scholarships were announced for three outstanding graduates to continue advanced Islamic studies.
